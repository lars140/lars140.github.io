Electric Guitar Basics

Your first 10 minutes starts now.

By the end of this lesson, you’ll know how to hold a pick, read a tab, and play the hook from Seven Nation Army on one string.

⚡

What you’ll need

  • An electric guitar
  • A pick (any thickness)
  • About 10 quiet minutes
✓

Today’s goal

Play a short riff using one string and simple fret numbers.

Step 1 · Single notes

Hold the pick like it’s a tiny key.

Rest it on the side of your index finger, then place your thumb over the top. Let just a small corner peek out.

Try this

  1. Rest your picking hand near the bridge.
  2. Pick the thickest string once, gently.
  3. Keep your wrist relaxed—small movement wins.

Step 2 · Guitar tabs

Tabs are a map for your fingers.

Each line is a string. The bottom line is the thick low E string. A number tells you which fret to press; 0 means play the string open.
